Property

Homes, cars and rentals: what they're worth, what's owed and when it's paid off. Set them up in Settings › Property.

Values are in dollars of each year. A home grows by its yearly rate; something with a lifetime (a car) goes down to its end value, then is replaced at today's price grown by inflation. Loans follow their payment until paid off.

Return & inflation
Pick a preset to set both sliders, then fine-tune.

Account balances are grown at the return rate, converted to today's dollars at the inflation rate, then turned into monthly income with the withdrawal rate (4% a year is a common rule of thumb). Pension estimates are treated as today's dollars. Income is compared with projected retirement spending from Spending over time below.
