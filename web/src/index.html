Assumptions

0% means pay just keeps up with inflation. Raises grow take-home pay each year until retirement and scale up pensions based on final pay.

Return & inflation
Tick a preset to set both sliders, then fine-tune.

Account balances are grown at the return rate, converted to today's dollars at the inflation rate, then turned into monthly income with the withdrawal rate (4% a year is a common rule of thumb). Pension estimates are treated as today's dollars. Income is compared with projected retirement spending from Spending over time below.
